# Digital-health platforms and equity in avoidable admissions

## Contesto e stato dell'arte

Evaluations of Italian digital-health platforms stop at single-region case studies and do not test whether the recorded reduction reaches low-income patients (Ministry of Health, hospital discharge records, 2024 release; MUR, PRIN 2026 evaluation guidelines, D.D. 2298 All. 3; OECD, Health at a Glance: Europe, 2024).

## Obiettivi

To what extent do regional digital-health platforms reduce avoidable hospital admissions for patients with chronic conditions, and how is that reduction distributed across income groups? Criteri: the reduction in avoidable admissions per 1,000 chronic patients; the ratio of that reduction in the lowest income quintile to the highest. Operazionalizzazione: Avoidable admissions are the ambulatory-care-sensitive conditions set, counted per 1,000 registered chronic patients per year; equity is the ratio of the platform effect in the lowest income quintile to the effect in the highest, with the quintile assigned from the patient's exemption category. Ambito: Six Italian regions that activated a platform between 2019 and 2023; patients aged 18 and over with at least one chronic condition; 2016 to 2026. Costituirebbe una risposta: The question is answered when a region-level effect on avoidable admissions is estimated against non-adopting regions and the equity ratio is reported with its confidence interval.

## Metodologia e piano di lavoro

Staggered difference-in-differences on administrative hospital records, with event-study checks and a synthetic control for the earliest adopter. Fonti: Hospital discharge records, 2016-2026, Ministry of Health (dati); Chronic-disease exemption registry, 2016-2026, regional health agencies (dati); Platform activation dates and covered services, 2019-2023, regional health agencies (dati).

Define the six treated regions and the eligible control regions; aggregate discharges to region-year cells by condition set and income quintile; estimate the effect of activation with region and year fixed effects; inspect event-study leads for pre-trends; repeat the estimate for each quintile and report the ratio; run a synthetic control for the 2019 adopter as a robustness check.

Condizioni di validità: Regions are compared before and after activation, with non-adopting regions as controls; Discharge coding stays stable across the period, checked on a fixed set of diagnoses; Income quintile is observed for every patient through the exemption category. Limiti: The design estimates the effect of activation, not of platform use intensity; Regions that activated before 2019 fall outside the comparison; Avoidable admissions measure access, not the clinical quality of ambulatory care; The equity ratio is sensitive to how patients without an exemption are assigned.

## Risultati attesi e impatto

Risultato atteso: Activation reduces avoidable admissions by between 3 and 6 per cent, and the reduction is smaller in the lowest income quintile than in the highest.

La procedura produce: An open dataset of region-level avoidable-admission rates by condition set and income quintile, 2016-2026, with the code that builds it.

Premesse normative: Equal access to care for equal need is a condition of a legitimate regional health service; A reduction in admissions that accrues only to the better-off does not count as an improvement. Criteri: the absolute reduction in avoidable admissions; the ratio between the lowest and the highest income quintile.
